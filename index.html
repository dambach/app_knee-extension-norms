PredKneeExt

Isometric knee extensor strength measured at 90° knee flexion, compared with three published reference equations. The result is given as % of predicted and z-score against the lower limit of normal.

Reference equations and test protocols

All three protocols test the knee at 90° of flexion. A result is only interpretable against a reference whose position, device and trial selection match the measurement protocol.
